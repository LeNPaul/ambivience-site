A scrapbook for everything you’ve saved.

Every app has an algorithm deciding what you see next. Ambivience lets you set your own. It gathers your Watch Later videos, bookmarks, Instagram saves and notes into one wall you curate, and brings them back in the order you pick.

That's everything.

Saving something feels like progress.

Then it sinks into Watch Later, a bookmarks folder, or a saved tab on Instagram. Meanwhile, most feeds are tuned to keep you looking for the next new thing.

Ambivience turns that around. Go back to what you already found worth keeping. Open it like a scrapbook you made, not a feed built to hold your attention.

You set the algorithm

Your algorithm comes down to two choices: what goes in a feed, and the order it comes back in. Filter by tag, source, or collection, then pick newest, oldest, random, or Resurface.

Resurface brings things back on a schedule that stretches as you go: a day after you see something, then two days, four, eight, and on up to six months. Nothing ranks your saves behind the scenes, and nothing learns from what you click.

Two reasons you saved it

To watch or read

The talk you meant to get to, the article for later. Mark it done and it leaves the wall. Not today? Skip it, and Resurface brings it back another day.

Done

To see again

The quote, the recipe, the room you'd like to live in. Mark one seen, and Resurface waits a little longer each time before it comes back.

Seen

Add a note to anything, and it comes back with the card every time. Your own writing goes on the wall too: a ratio, a packing list, a line you don't want to forget.

Done and archived saves wait on their own page, and you can bring any of them back. Search finds a half-remembered save by its title, note, site, or tags.

Every card is a note in your vault

One saved thing is one markdown file in an Ambivience folder inside your vault. Open it in Obsidian, add tags, link it from other notes, rename it, move it. The wall keeps up.

  • Properties you add yourself are kept exactly as you wrote them.
  • Edit a note in Obsidian while the wall is open and the card updates on its own.
  • Delete sends the file to your vault's trash, never straight to oblivion.
  • Stop using Ambivience and you lose nothing. The notes are still yours.

Bring in what you've already saved

YouTube
Watch Later and your playlists, from a Google Takeout export. Watch Later comes in as things to watch, other playlists as things to see again.
Instagram
Saved posts and collections, from your Instagram data download.
Bookmarks
Exported bookmarks from Chrome, Firefox, or Safari, with folders kept as collections. Safari's Reading List comes in as things to read.
Links in your notes
Already keep reading lists in Obsidian? Pick the notes or folders, and Ambivience copies the links out of them. It reads only the notes you pick, and never changes them.
A list of links
Paste them in, one per line.

Nothing is written until you've looked it over. Keep the collections you want, untick the rest, and decide what's for watching and what's for keeping. Links you already have are recognized, even when one is a youtu.be link and the other isn't. Titles, thumbnails, and publish dates are filled in for you.

Save from your phone with Obsidian

Make a new note in the Ambivience folder and paste a link on the first line. Anything you write below it becomes your note. When the wall next opens, it's a card.

What it leaves out, on purpose

  • No account, no cloud

    Ambivience runs on your own computer and keeps everything in one folder of your vault. There's nothing to sign up for.

  • No counters or streaks

    No unread numbers, badges, red dots, or notifications. It's there when you open it.

  • No ranking you can't see

    Your feeds show what you asked for, in the order you set.

  • No scraping or passwords

    Imports use the export files YouTube, Instagram, and your browser already give you.

Opening it the first time

Ambivience isn't signed with a paid developer certificate, so your computer asks before opening it the first time. You do this once per version.

macOS 15 or later
Open Ambivience, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to the message about Ambivience and click Open Anyway.
macOS 13 or 14
Right-click Ambivience in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.
Windows
If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Ambivience then opens in your browser and asks where your vault is. On a Mac it sits in the menu bar. On Windows and Linux, quit it from the ••• menu in the app.

Updating, uninstalling, and checking your download are covered in the install guide.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for personal use. Download it and run it on your own computers at no cost. The source code isn't published.

Does anything leave my computer?

Your saves stay in your vault. To fill in titles and thumbnails, Ambivience visits the pages you saved, the way your browser would. Nothing else is sent anywhere, and the app only answers on your own computer.

Is there any algorithm?

Only the one you set. Each feed shows what matches your filters, in the order you chose. Nothing is ranked by what holds your attention.

Why does my computer warn me before opening it?

Ambivience isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer certificate, so macOS and Windows ask you to confirm the first time you open each version. Each release lists checksums, so you can confirm your download is exactly what was published. The install guide shows how.

Will it remind me about things?

It never sends notifications. Choose the Resurface order and things come back on a growing schedule: a day after you see one, then two days, four, eight, and so on, up to six months. Skipping something you haven't got to yet works the same way.

Do I need Obsidian?

Ambivience works on a folder of markdown files, so any folder will do. Obsidian is the easy way to edit notes, add tags, and save from your phone.

Can I open the wall on my phone?

Not in version 1. The wall runs on your computer. On your phone, save into the Ambivience folder with Obsidian and the card will be waiting when you next open the wall.

Why don't my Instagram saves have pictures?

Instagram's export doesn't include images, so Instagram saves show as cards with the account name, the collection, and your note. Tap through to see the post.

How do I update it?

Download the new version and install it over the old one. Your vault and settings stay as they are. The install guide has the steps for each system.

What happens to my notes if I stop using it?

Nothing. They're plain markdown files in your vault, and they stay there, readable in Obsidian or any text editor.
